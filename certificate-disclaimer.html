2. Use of Publicly Available Endpoints

This utility does not bypass security walls, hack, or decrypt any restricted systems. It simply formats and navigates to the public citizen certificate routing endpoint (https://edistrict.odisha.gov.in/t/<TOKEN>) that the official Odisha e-District portal provides in SMS receipts to applicants.

All certificates retrieved originate directly from the government servers to the end-user's web browser.

3. Anti-Spam & Fair Use Requirement
